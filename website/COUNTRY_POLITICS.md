# EarnWage — political and territorial country profiles

This module displays **descriptive institutional categories**, not a democracy index, governance score, electoral rating or an assessment of the sitting government.

The fourteen country profiles have three separate dimensions:

- **Form of state**: constitutional monarchy or republic.
- **System of government**: parliamentary, semi-presidential, presidential or collegiate/directorial.
- **Territorial organisation**: federal, unitary, or a more precise unitary category where constitutional autonomy/devolution matters.

## Sources and reference periods

For PT, ES, DE, FR, IE, NL and IT, each record links to the corresponding official European Union country profile (checked 2026-11-07).

For GB, CH, US, CA, BR, IN and PK, the link points to the **archived 2025 CIA World Factbook government-type catalogue**, republished at https://www.thefactbook.org/fields/government-type. These records are labelled **2025**, and are *not* advertised as live government updates in 2026. Review against the constitution and official government sites before commercial publication.

The website provides translations in EN/PT-PT/ES/DE/FR/IT/NL and uses the existing country selection and language selection. There is no change to API, production SQLite data or country indicator definitions.
