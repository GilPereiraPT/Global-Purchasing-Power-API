Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is purchasing power parity (PPP)?

PPP adjusts monetary amounts using relative national price levels. Here we use private-consumption PPP, in local currency per international dollar. It is not a bank exchange rate or a city-level price index.

Does EarnWage calculate my exact take-home pay?

Not by default. Personal taxes depend on residence, household circumstances and local rules. Where explicit validated scenarios exist, estimates are labelled separately; incomplete deductions are never presented as net income.

Where do the salary data come from?

EarnWage uses attributed statistical sources and job providers with their original units, locations and reference periods. A published job salary is different from a national occupational average.

Can I use EarnWage on my phone?

Yes, this site is designed for mobile browsers. The Android app is being developed alongside the web platform.
